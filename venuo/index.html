Your location stays yours.

Venuo never shows your real-time location to anyone. GPS is only used to see what's near you — never to expose you. Privacy by design, and payments always processed securely server-side.

Questions

What venues ask us.

Can I start today?

If you run a venue, yes: create your account, publish your menu, and start taking orders. The customer app comes later — meanwhile your room and your payments already work.

What does it cost?

For people, nothing. For businesses there's a subscription and a per-order fee — you see both when you create the account, before connecting anything.

Does it work in my city?

The dashboard, the menu and the payments work from anywhere. What grows city by city is the customer base — and it grows where there are active venues.

Do I have to share my location?

GPS is only used to show you what's nearby. Venuo never shows your real-time location to anyone — not to other users, not to venues.

I have a venue. What do I need to start?

Nothing to install. A Stripe account to receive payments, and your menu. Setup takes minutes from the dashboard.
