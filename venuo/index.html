Questions

What people ask before joining.

When can I actually use Venuo?

It's being built right now. We open city by city, and the waitlist goes in first. We're not publishing a date we can't yet guarantee.

What does it cost?

For people, nothing. For businesses, pricing is announced at launch — founding venues get preferential terms that we honor afterwards.

Which cities are you opening in?

We prioritize by where the most people and venues sign up. If you register a venue, the city you give us counts toward that decision.

Do I have to share my location?

GPS is only used to show you what's nearby. Venuo never shows your real-time location to anyone — not to other users, not to venues.

I have a venue. What do I need to start?

Nothing to install. A Stripe account to receive payments, and your menu. Setup takes minutes from the dashboard.

What do you do with my email?

Store it to notify you once, when we open where you live. We don't sell it, don't share it, and don't put you in any marketing sequence.
