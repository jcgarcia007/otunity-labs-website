Protocol

Four phases, in this order, always.

The order is what prevents the two surprises that ruin a project: the one on the invoice and the one at delivery. Nobody writes a line of code before a signed document exists with scope, risks, timeline and price.

PHASE 01

Sample

We sit down with whoever does the work today. We leave with the problem written on one page, with numbers.

1–2 weeks
PHASE 02

Diagnosis

Scope, deliverables, risks, timeline and a fixed price. If it isn't worth doing, we say so here and there's no invoice.

1 week
PHASE 03

Culture

Every two weeks you get something working in your hands, not a status report. If the direction is wrong, you find out early.

Scope-dependent
PHASE 04

Handover

Code, access, documentation and a session with your team. Ongoing support if you want it; everything keeps running if you don't.

2 weeks

Working terms

The guarantees, in writing.
